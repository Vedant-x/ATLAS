# Comparing ATLAS with other services

No comparison has been run yet. ATLAS makes no claim to beat HOF, Outlier, OddsJam, Rithmm or any
other service. A claim like that needs the protocol below, run prospectively, and is scored with
`js/compare.js`.

## Protocol (fixed before the first selection)

1. **Scope.** The same sports, competitions and dates for every service, and the same odds band
   (for example 1.70–2.30 for singles near 2.00).
2. **Deadline.** Every service's selections are recorded before the same cut-off (for example 60 minutes
   before the start). Later changes do not count.
3. **Selection procedure.** Written down for each service in advance: its published pick, or for a
   research tool, a stated rule such as "the top-rated selection in band". ATLAS does not invent an
   "official pick" for a tool that publishes none.
4. **Prices.** The price actually available at the deadline, with the bookmaker and the time. A
   consensus or average is never used as the price.
5. **Stakes.** One unit on every selection, for every service.
6. **Completeness.** Every published selection is kept. Every opportunity in scope that a service
   passed on is counted as an abstention.
7. **Singles and parlays** are scored separately.
8. **Disclosure.** Subscription costs, fees, exclusions and any manual recording are stated next to the
   results.
9. **Sources.** Authorised exports, or manual recording with screenshots kept.

## Evidence labels (never merged into one ranking)

- Independently audited
- Reproduced from a complete ledger
- Provider-reported
- User testimonial
- Historical backtest

## What is reported

- Opportunities, selections and abstentions.
- Average and quartile odds.
- Wins, losses, pushes and voids.
- Profit at 1 unit per selection.
- ROI with a 95% interval.
- Price against the closing line.
- Brier score where probabilities are published.
- Maximum drawdown.
- Breakdowns by sport, market and model version.

**Ranking.** Two services are ranked only within one evidence category and one kind (singles or
parlays), and only when their 95% ROI intervals do not overlap. Otherwise the result is
"insufficient evidence to rank". A fixed number of picks is not enough on its own.
